35 /// The material shares of `terrain` at world (x, y): soil, then bedrock where the ground beneath
36 /// the first `MaterialSource` layer is bare, then dust in patches, then each `MaterialSource`
37 /// layer's cover in layer order, each read on the ground beneath it.
38 /// @param spacing Sample spacing in meters; positive.
39 /// @throws std::invalid_argument as `ValidateMaterialOptions`.
40 MaterialShares MaterialsAt(const Terrain& terrain, const MaterialOptions& options, double x,
41 double y, double spacing);

43 /// One raster per `MaterialClass` over `grid`, each cell `MaterialsAt` its center at the grid's
44 /// spacing (or `evaluation.spacing` where coarser), on `evaluation.threads` threads to the same
45 /// bits as one. Material layers read the ground beneath them on `evaluation.backend`, and
46 /// `evaluation.onLayer` is called after each with its index and time; the stage cache is unused.
47 /// @throws std::invalid_argument as `MaterialsAt`, or for a negative or non-finite spacing.
48 /// @throws std::runtime_error when a GPU backend is requested but unavailable
49 /// (`ResolveBackend`).
