43 /// A grid of heights as a mesh: a vertex at every cell center with data, and two triangles facing
44 /// up for every square of four such vertices outside the cutouts, with a skirt facing out below
45 /// each boundary edge. Positions are in the frame boulder models are written in: world
46 /// coordinates and heights on a plane; on a sphere, meters east, north and up of `site` on the
47 /// datum sphere (as `PlacementAtSite`), so terrain and boulders line up. Skirts hang down the
48 /// frame's up axis. Made with `GetMeshSize` and `FillMesh`.
49 /// @param site Surface coordinates of the site; unused on a plane.
50 /// @throws std::invalid_argument as `FillMesh`.

60 /// Writes the mesh `BuildMesh` makes of `heights` into caller buffers, bit for bit: `positions`
61 /// as x, y, z triples and `triangles` as vertex index triples, in `BuildMesh`'s order. The layout

63 /// and (M, 3) int32, so callers resize those to `GetMeshSize` and pass their data with no copy.
64 /// @param positions 3 * `GetMeshSize(heights, options).vertices` doubles, in meters.
65 /// @param triangles 3 * `GetMeshSize(heights, options).triangles` indices into the vertices.

67 /// @throws std::invalid_argument as `GetMeshSize`, for a buffer of another size, or for a mesh of
