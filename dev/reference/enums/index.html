    Enumerations¶

    The named values fields choose between.

    aging¶

    Value Meaning
    freshness Blocks wear away with the crater's freshness (current over fresh depth): all when fresh, none at or below the minimum freshness.
    age Blocks wear away with the crater's age as (1 - age / lifetime)^exponent, synthterrain's form.

    basis¶

    Value Meaning
    perlin Improved Perlin gradient noise: smooth, general-purpose relief.
    simplex Simplex gradient noise: smooth relief, an alternative to Perlin.
    value Value noise: random values interpolated over a lattice; blockier than gradient noise.
    worley_f1 Cellular noise from the distance to the nearest feature point: cones and basins.
    worley_f2 Cellular noise from the distance to the second-nearest feature point.
    worley_f2_minus_f1 Cellular noise F2 - F1: ridges along cell borders, like cracked ground.
    voronoi Voronoi cells, each at one random height: flat polygonal plateaus and terraces.

    blend¶

    Value Meaning
    add Adds the layer to the height so far.
    max Keeps the higher of the height so far and the layer.
    min Keeps the lower of the height so far and the layer.
    mask Multiplies the height so far by (layer / amplitude + 1) / 2, so the layer modulates roughness by region.

    chronology¶

    Value Meaning
    neukum Neukum et al. (2001) lunar chronology, for surfaces 0 to 4.6 Gyr old.
    ivanov Ivanov (2001) Martian chronology, for surfaces 0 to 4.6 Gyr old.

    compression¶

    Value Meaning
    none Uncompressed: the largest files.
    lzw Lossless LZW compression, widely supported.
    deflate Lossless Deflate (zlib) compression.
    packbits PackBits run-length compression; read only, so outputs cannot use it.

    curve¶

    Value Meaning
    minimum The model's minimum curve: the fewest boulders.
    nominal The model's nominal curve.
    maximum The model's maximum curve: the most boulders.

    datum¶

    Value Meaning
    none No datum: the crater profile is added onto the terrain everywhere.
    plane Interior cut relative to a least-squares plane over the fit footprint, keeping regional slopes.
    mean_height Interior cut relative to a flat datum at the mean height over the fit footprint.
    center_height Interior cut relative to a flat datum at the terrain height under the crater center.

    diffusivity¶

    Value Meaning
    matched With steady-state ages, the diffusivity that wears each crater to Fassett et al.'s (2022) visibility cutoff, depth 0.04 of the diameter, at the end of its equilibrium lifetime, as they derive it, for the layer's own profile; fassett_2022_hartmann for other ages.
    fassett_2022_hartmann Lunar size-dependent diffusivity of Fassett et al. (2022) for the Hartmann equilibrium, 3.6 D^0.87 m^2/Myr with D in km.
    fassett_2022_trask Lunar size-dependent diffusivity of Fassett et al. (2022) for the Trask equilibrium, 3.1 D^1.05 m^2/Myr with D in km.
    fassett_thomson_2014 Lunar diffusivity of Fassett and Thomson (2014), 5.5 m^2/Myr for every crater size; it erases craters below a few hundred meters within a small share of their lifetimes.
    fassett_thomson_2014_calibration Fassett and Thomson's (2014) lunar diffusion age against crater age, independent of size; suits kilometer-scale craters.
    power_law A diffusivity of your own, coefficient (D / 1 km)^exponent m^2/Myr, from the power_law settings.

    equilibrium¶

    Value Meaning
    trask Trask (1966) lunar equilibrium, N(>=D) = 10^-1.1 D^-2, as the DSNE uses.
    hartmann Hartmann (1984) lunar equilibrium, N(>=D) = 10^-1.14 D^-1.83 per km^2 with D in km.

    fade_shape¶

    Value Meaning
    linear Straight ramp: continuous, with kinks at both ends.
    smoothstep Cubic 3t^2 - 2t^3: continuous slope across the transition.
    smootherstep Quintic 6t^5 - 15t^4 + 10t^3: continuous slope and curvature, the smoothest join.
    gaussian Gaussian falloff with sigma a third of the band, rescaled to end at zero.

    format¶

    Value Meaning
    csv Comma-separated values, one row per item.
    json A JSON document.

    fractal¶

    Value Meaning
    fbm Fractional Brownian motion: a weighted sum of octaves, the general-purpose choice.
    ridged Ridged multifractal: sharp crests from inverted absolute noise, like eroded ridges.
    billow Sum of absolute-value octaves: rounded, puffy forms.
    hybrid_multifractal Hybrid multifractal: smooth lowlands and rough highlands.

    interp¶

    Value Meaning
    bilinear Square nodes the mean of their four corners, diamond nodes the mean of their four neighbors, samples bilinear between nodes.
    catmull_rom Catmull-Rom splines over four nodes along each axis, as the VIPER terrain expansion does: smoother, with fewer lattice creases.

    kind¶

    Value Meaning
    constant Always a.
    uniform Uniform between a and b.
    normal Normal with mean a and standard deviation b.
    log_normal Log-normal with median a and standard deviation b of the natural logarithm.
    piecewise Uniform within each bin between edges, each bin drawn in proportion to its weight.

    measure¶

    Value Meaning
    scale The local density over the layer's own; 1 keeps it as the size distribution gives it.
    count Features with diameters in the map's range per area unit, such as boulders per km^2.
    areal_fraction Share of the ground covered by features in the map's range, such as rock abundance.

    measure¶

    Value Meaning
    exponent The cumulative exponent alpha, counts falling as D^-alpha.
    mean_diameter The mean diameter in meters of the features from the reference diameter up, from which alpha = mean / (mean - D0).

    method¶

    Value Meaning
    nearest The value of the nearest cell center: blocky, but never mixes values.
    bilinear Linear in each axis over the four surrounding cells.
    cubic Keys cubic convolution (a = -0.5) over the sixteen surrounding cells: smooth, for upscaling DEMs.
    catmull_rom Another name for cubic: the Catmull-Rom spline is the same kernel.
    lanczos3 The three-lobe Lanczos kernel over the 36 surrounding cells: sharper than cubic, with slight ringing at steps.
    cubic_bspline The cubic B-spline over the sixteen surrounding cells: the smoothest, but it smooths the cells themselves.

    mode¶

    Value Meaning
    none Each cell takes the first source with data, unchanged: a step where a gap begins.
    feather Adds the height difference at the nearest edge post to the later source, fading it to 0 across width_m of the gap: the cheapest, without a solve.
    height Shifts the later source by the edge's height difference spread smoothly into the gap, fading out over decay_length_m beyond an open edge: heights meet (C0), keeping the later source's relief.
    slope As height, with a biharmonic spread that matches slopes at the edge as well (C1).

    model¶

    Value Meaning
    power_law Rim harmonics with amplitudes falling as a power of the harmonic number, of a given RMS: a simple, configurable spectrum.
    du_2024 The fitted rim-crest spectra of fresh lunar craters of Du et al. (2024), from each crater's diameter.

    model¶

    Value Meaning
    intercrater 0.0001 D^-1.75457 per m^2 with D in meters: synthetic_moon's "low blockiness".
    haworth 0.002 D^-2.6607 per m^2 with D in meters: synthetic_moon's "high blockiness".
    viper_specification 0.0003 D^-2.482 per m^2 with D in meters: the VIPER Environmental Specification.

    model¶

    Value Meaning
    nearest One center per cell of a jittered lattice, each point weighted by its nearest center only.
    thomas A Thomas cluster process: Poisson-scattered centers whose Gaussian clusters add where they overlap.

    morphometry¶

    Value Meaning
    lunar Fresh lunar craters: Fassett et al. (2022) depth and rim height below 15 km, Pike's (1977) complex crater fits above.
    mars Fresh Martian craters: depth and rim height capped for small craters, Garvin et al.'s (2003) fits for large and complex craters from 7 km.

    order¶

    Value Meaning
    oldest_first Oldest craters first, then larger first: the physical order, so younger craters overprint older ones.
    largest_first Largest craters first, then older first, so smaller craters overprint larger ones.

    production¶

    Value Meaning
    neukum Neukum et al. (2001) lunar production function, valid from 10 m to 300 km.
    extended_neukum The Neukum production function continued below 10 m as a power law of slope -2.85, valid from 2.5 m to 300 km.
    ivanov Ivanov (2001) Martian production function, valid from 15 m to 362 km.

    profile¶

    Value Meaning
    smoothstep Every block inside the rim, falling along a smoothstep to none at the reach.
    exponential Exponential decay outside the rim, a fixed share on the floor and none beyond the reach: synthterrain's crater probability map.

    reference¶

    Value Meaning
    none No parameters: each class present must be given.
    mars_pancam Pancam fits at 753 nm (Johnson et al. 2006a, b, tabulated by Fernando et al. 2013): soil, sand, dust, bedrock (Eagle crater outcrop, Johnson et al. 2006b) and rock.

    relief¶

    Value Meaning
    dome Each boulder's exposed part drawn as a dome: cheap, and enough for small boulders.
    shape The top surface of each boulder's 3D boulder shape drawn into the heights.
    none No rock relief in the heights, only fillets when set, for programs that place every boulder as a model of its own.

    rest¶

    Value Meaning
    face On its broadest face: its short axis up.
    side On a side: its intermediate axis up.
    end On an end: its long axis up.

    rim¶

    Value Meaning
    original The published fits, whose interior and exterior meet at 0.98 radii.
    at_one_radius Interior fit extended to 1.0 radii so the rim lies at the stated diameter, as synthterrain's FT_Crater; leaves a small step at the rim.
    plateau A flat rim at the fresh rim height from 0.98 to 1.02 radii, after Fassett et al. (2022), as synthterrain's FTmod_Crater.

    shape¶

    Value Meaning
    barchan Isolated crescents with a slip face and horns downwind; domes below min_width_m.
    dome Isolated rounded heaps without a slip face.
    transverse Continuous crests across the wind, each with a slip face.
    barchanoid Transverse crests bent into linked crescents, as where sand supply rises.
    linear Symmetric crests along the wind, as under two winds on scarce sand.

    solid¶

    Value Meaning
    icosahedron Boulders start from an icosahedron, twenty triangles on the unit sphere: rounder shapes.
    cube Boulders start from a cube: blocky shapes.
    pyramid Boulders start from a square-based pyramid.
    prism Boulders start from a triangular prism, a wedge.
    tetrahedron Boulders start from a regular tetrahedron: angular shapes.

    terrain¶

    Value Meaning
    rough_mare Moore's rough mare crater distribution: 10^-1 D^-2 per m^2 below 100 m, 10 D^-3 above.
    smooth_mare Moore's smooth mare crater distribution: 10^-1 D^-2 per m^2 below 40 m, then D^-3, D^-1.68 from 100 m and D^-3 from 200 m.
    uplands Moore's uplands crater distribution: 10^-1 D^-2 per m^2 below 1 km, 10^2 D^-3 above.
    heavily_cratered Moore's heavily cratered distribution: 10^-1 D^-2 per m^2 up to 10 km.
