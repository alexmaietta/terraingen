# Workflows

Detailed walkthroughs and tutorials that take several steps, with the choices along the way, such as upscaling a DEM of a landing site or preparing terrain for a rover simulation. The [Quickstart](../../getting-started/quickstart.md) walks through a first terrain and the [tasks](../index.md#tasks) show single steps.

- [Customize TerrainGen](customize-terraingen.md) replaces each part of a terrain with one of your own, from sources and layers to upsamplers, crater models, density maps, albedo and rock meshes.
