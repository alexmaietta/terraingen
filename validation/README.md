# Validation

We want to make sure TerrainGen can produce accurate results and products. To that end, we can leverage existing map products and orbital data to validate that TerrainGen produces reasonable results.

- Take NAC images and low resolution base terrain and then upscale the low resolution terrain and compare resulting terrain
- Take Apollo images and existing map products at those sites and upscale to show improvements and accuracy at the surface level
- Take something like low resolution LDEM areas and try and reproduce higher resolution LDEMs and compare resulting visuals and distributions
