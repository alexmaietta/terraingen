    Albedo layers¶

    Chosen by "type" in layers.

    Type Description
    craters Bright ejecta, interiors and rays of the craters of a crater layer, fading with crater age.
    splotches Dark and bright splotches that secondary impacts of loose regolith leave over the whole surface, after Speyerer et al.
    blast_zones Brighter zones of regolith around landers scoured by their descent engines, after Clegg et al.
    rocks Rock brighter than the regolith: the boulders of boulder layers where resolved, and a speckle of the share of each sample covered by those too small to resolve.
    slopes Steep ground kept immature and bright by regolith moving downslope, after Ikeda et al.

    craters¶

    Bright ejecta, interiors and rays of the craters of a crater layer, fading with crater age.

    crater_layer

    integer, optional · default null

    Which top-level crater layer's craters to brighten, counting from 0; unset uses every top-level crater layer.

    seed

    integer · default 0

    Seed of which craters get bright material, their rays and their impacts; changing it gives a different pattern of the same statistics.

    min_diameter_m

    number · default 0.0 · unit m

    Smallest diameter, in meters, of craters given bright material. Must not be negative.

    max_diameter_m

    number, optional · default null · unit m

    Largest diameter, in meters, of craters given bright material; unset has no upper limit. Must not be negative.

    min_age_gyr

    number · default 0.0 · unit Gyr

    Youngest age, in Gyr, of craters given bright material. Must not be negative.

    max_age_gyr

    number, optional · default null · unit Gyr

    Oldest age, in Gyr, of craters given bright material; unset has no upper limit. Must not be negative.

    manual

    boolean · default true

    Gives manual craters bright material too; otherwise only generated and replacing craters have it.

    share

    number · default 1.0

    Share of eligible craters given bright material. Must be from 0 to 1.

    background_maturity

    number · default 0.202

    Optical maturity (OMAT) of the ground around the craters; the excesses are added to it and the albedo is brightened from it. Must be positive.

    max_maturity

    number · default 0.42

    Cap on the optical maturity where craters' excesses add up, at least the background; it limits how bright overlapping craters get.

    interior_excess

    number · default 0.132

    Optical maturity above the background inside a fresh crater's rim; higher values give brighter interiors. Must not be negative.

    ejecta_excess

    number · default 0.076

    Optical maturity above the background on a fresh crater's continuous ejecta at the rim, falling with distance. Must not be negative.

    ejecta_exponent

    number · default 3.0

    Exponent of the ejecta's fall with distance r, as (r / R)^-exponent for crater radius R; higher values keep the bright ejecta nearer the rim. Must be positive.

    ejecta_reach_radii

    number · default 3.0

    Distance from the center, in crater radii, at which the ejecta's excess reaches 0. Must be above 1.

    fade_exponent

    number · default 1.0

    Exponent of the fade with age t, max(0, 1 - t / T)^exponent for retention time T; 1 is a straight fade and higher values fade sooner. Must be positive.

    origin_reflectance

    number · default 0.08

    Reflectance of the optical maturity origin, the hypermature end-member, from which reflectance scales with maturity at fixed iron content.

    fade_px

    list of number · default [...] · unit px

    How bright material of craters too small to resolve at the sample spacing fades out. Each must not be negative.

    fade_shape

    one of linear, smoothstep, smootherstep, gaussian · default "smoothstep"

    Curve of the fade between the two sample counts of fade_px.

    texture: Filaments, lobed ejecta edges and walls brighter than floors; off unless streaks, lobes or a floor share below 1 are set.
    streaks

    number · default 0.0

    Share of the excess radial streaks take away between filaments, breaking ejecta, rays and interiors into fine streaks; 0 leaves them smooth. Must be from 0 to 1.

    streak_width_deg

    number · default 3.0 · unit deg

    Typical width of a filament around the crater, in degrees. Must be positive.

    streak_length_radii

    number · default 1.5

    Typical length of a filament outward from the crater, in crater radii. Must be positive.

    continuous_radii

    number · default 0.0

    How far beyond the rim the ejecta stay continuous before breaking into filaments, in crater radii; 0 streaks them from the rim. Must not be negative.

    lobes

    number · default 0.0

    How far the ejecta's edge wanders in and out, as a share of its reach; 0 keeps it circular. Must be from 0 to 0.9.

    lobe_count

    integer · default 8

    Typical number of lobes around a crater. Must be from 1 to 64.

    floor_share

    number · default 1.0

    Interior excess on the floor over that at the rim; 1 keeps the interior even, lower values brighten the walls over the floor. Must be from 0 to 1.

    seed

    integer · default 0

    Varies every crater's texture.

    zones: Bright and dark reflectance zones around new small craters, after Robinson et al. (2015) and Speyerer et al. (2016); off by default.
    enabled

    boolean · default false

    Gives new small craters the reflectance zones of LROC temporal pairs; off by default.

    max_diameter_m

    number · default 50.0 · unit m

    Largest crater diameter, in meters, with zones; the published fits stop near 43 m. Must be positive.

    mature_depth_m

    number · default 1.0 · unit m

    Depth in meters of mature regolith; only craters excavating deeper get the proximal high reflectance zone. Must not be negative.

    excavation_ratio

    number · default 0.1

    Excavation depth over crater diameter. Must be positive.

    seed

    integer · default 0

    Varies every crater's zones.

    proximal_high: Proximal high reflectance zone: immature ejecta, +12% to a median 1.5 D.
    share

    number · default 1.0

    Share of craters with the zone. Must be from 0 to 1.

    coefficient_m

    number · default 1.0 · unit m

    Coefficient of the zone's reach from the crater's center, coefficient D^exponent meters for diameter D in meters. Must be positive.

    exponent

    number · default 1.0

    Exponent of the diameter in the zone's reach. Must be positive.

    raggedness

    number · default 0.0

    Share of the reach that fingers of the edge pull in by; 0 keeps the zone round, higher values make it ray-like. Must be from 0 to below 1.

    finger_width_deg

    number · default 10.0 · unit deg

    Typical width of the edge's fingers around the crater, in degrees. Must be positive.

    feather

    number · default 0.3

    Share of the reach over which the zone fades out at its edge. Must be above 0 and at most 1.

    retention_myr

    number, optional · default null · unit Myr

    How long the zone lasts, in Myr, fading like the bright material; unset lasts as long as the bright material. Must be positive.

    change: Reflectance change relative to the pre-impact surface, drawn per crater; above -1, negative values darken.
    kind

    one of constant, uniform, normal, log_normal, piecewise · default "constant"

    Kind of distribution the value is drawn from.

    a

    number · default 0.0

    The constant value, the uniform's lower bound, the normal's mean or the log-normal's median.

    b

    number · default 0.0

    The uniform's upper bound, or the standard deviation (of the natural logarithm for a log-normal); unused for a constant.

    min

    number, optional · default null

    Lower bound drawn values are clamped to; unset leaves them unbounded below.

    max

    number, optional · default null

    Upper bound drawn values are clamped to; unset leaves them unbounded above, which normal and log-normal kinds do not allow.

    edges

    list of number · default []

    With piecewise, the edges of the bins, at least two and strictly increasing; values are uniform within each bin. Empty for every other kind.

    weights

    list of number · default []

    With piecewise, the relative weight of each bin, one fewer than the edges and not all zero; each bin draws its share of the total. Empty for every other kind. Each must not be negative.

    scale: Factor on the reach, drawn per crater; positive and bounded, a wider distribution gives more varied zones.

    splotches¶

    Dark and bright splotches that secondary impacts of loose regolith leave over the whole surface, after Speyerer et al. (2016).

    seed

    integer · default 0

    Seed of where splotches fall and what they are.

    rate_coefficient

    number · default 1.483e-11

    Coefficient a of the cumulative formation rate a d^-b per km^2 per year, d the diameter in km; higher values give more splotches. Must be positive.

    rate_exponent

    number · default 4.144

    Slope b of the cumulative formation rate; higher values give relatively more small splotches. Must be positive.

    lifetime_myr

    number · default 0.081 · unit Myr

    How long a splotch lasts in Myr, fading linearly; the default is the time splotches take to churn the top centimeters. Must be positive.

    min_diameter_m

    number · default 1.0 · unit m

    Smallest splotch diameter in meters; the published fit is extrapolated below 10 m. Must be positive.

    max_diameter_m

    number · default 40.0 · unit m

    Largest splotch diameter in meters, above the smallest. Must be positive.

    dark_share

    number · default 0.907

    Share of splotches darker than the ground. Must be from 0 to 1.

    bright_share

    number · default 0.074

    Share of splotches brighter than the ground; the rest are mixed, dark on one side and bright on the other. Must be from 0 to 1.

    steep_bright_share

    number, optional · default null

    Share of splotches on slopes steeper than the steep slope that are bright, read from the terrain's heights; unset keeps the bright share. Bright splotches are found mostly on such slopes, but the source gives no share. Must be from 0 to 1.

    steep_slope_deg

    number · default 15.0 · unit deg

    Slope in degrees from which ground is steep. Must be above 0 and below 90 deg.

    softness

    raggedness

    rocks¶

    Rock brighter than the regolith: the boulders of boulder layers where resolved, and a speckle of the share of each sample covered by those too small to resolve.

    boulder_layer

    integer, optional · default null

    Which top-level boulder layer's rock to brighten, counting from 0; unset uses every top-level boulder layer.

    seed

    integer · default 0

    Seed of the per-sample draws of rock too small to resolve.

    reflectance_ratio

    number · default 2.5

    Rock reflectance over the regolith's; 2.5 after Marshal et al. (2023), within Holt's (1981) Apollo 16 rocks at 18 to 51% against soils of 14 to 15%; earlier models used 1.5. Must be positive.

    unresolved

    boolean · default true

    Brightens each sample by the share of it covered by boulders too small to resolve, drawn about the expected share with the scatter of randomly placed rocks: bright speckle.

    slopes¶

    Steep ground kept immature and bright by regolith moving downslope, after Ikeda et al. (2022) and Lucey et al. (2014).

    onset_deg

    number · default 20.0 · unit deg

    Slope in degrees from which ground freshens; 20, where Lucey et al. (2014) and Ikeda et al. (2022) find brightening begin. Must be from 0 to below 90 deg.

    maturity_per_degree

    number · default 0.0065

    Optical maturity (OMAT) gained per degree beyond the onset; about 0.0065 on the crater walls of Ikeda et al. (2022). Must not be negative.

    background_maturity

    number · default 0.1

    OMAT of gentler ground; about 0.10 at 20 degrees in Laue (Ikeda et al. 2022). Must be positive.

    max_maturity

    number · default 0.42

    Cap on OMAT; at least the background's. Must be positive.

    baseline_m

    number · default 10.0 · unit m

    Meters slopes are measured over, the spacing of the lattice they are read on; 10, the DTM Ikeda et al. (2022) measured slopes on. Must be positive.

    origin_reflectance

    number · default 0.08

    Reflectance of the optical maturity origin, from which reflectance scales with maturity at fixed iron content.
